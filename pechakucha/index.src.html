    What changed from the Gemini draft

    Seven corrections and why
    • Structure. You asked for the brain, personality and nature versus nurture. Those now take slides 2 to 5; Gemini's draft gave them two slides. All three points from the course outline are still covered.
    • "Genetics play a small role." Not accurate. The slide now uses the Dunedin study, where the gene mattered only together with childhood maltreatment.
    • Micro-expressions and body language. Gemini's script used them for lie detection, and later in the same chat said no such tell exists. The script now uses false confessions and the PEACE interview model.
    • Organised and disorganised offenders. Dropped. The FBI typology has weak research support; method versus signature is clearer and safer.
    • "Krugman/Rossmo model." The geographic profiling model is Kim Rossmo's. There is no Krugman in it. It is not in the script; avoid the name in discussion.
    • "Trauma permanently damages the brain", "nobody is born a monster", "as easy as drinking a glass of water". Overstated or stock lines. Replaced with one named case and one named study per slide.
    • Speaker notes. The course rules forbid any text in the notes field, so the file is built with the notes empty. Save the script separately with the button below.
